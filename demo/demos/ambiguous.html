What happens when the page moves under a decision.

A compiled skill records, at every step, the words it saw and the place it found them in. Replay against changed data is the point — the fourth invoice is not the first. But when the place no longer carries those words and something else does, the two disagree about where the step is. Acting anyway would be guessing with the runtime's name on it, so the step is refused and both sides are named.

step 26: refused rather than guessed — 9 elements on this page fit «Field 26» about equally well (best 0.50), and the place this step stood in is gone
What follows it is the run repairing itself with a model — 26 model calls where a from-scratch agent needed 53, 23.6 s where it needed 80.4 s — and every repair is recorded like any other decision. A refusal is not a crash here. It is the point at which the runtime stops acting on its own record and starts asking.
