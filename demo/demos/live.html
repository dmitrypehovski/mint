    Why this is the honest version of a demo

    A recorded video proves that something worked once, under conditions the person who recorded it chose. A page that runs the real thing in front of you proves what happened just then — including when it goes wrong, which it does: the third task runs on a page it has never seen and occasionally has to ask a model about a step, and you will see that in the counters rather than read that it was smoothed out. The run writes a signed log as it goes, and the button at the end checks it with the same verifier a stranger would use.

    what this does not show

    It does not show that it will work on your page. Three repositories with clean, well-marked structure are the easy case, and these are all such pages. It also does not show that the first run was free: the skill running here was learned once with a model in the loop, and that cost is paid before any of this. What you are watching is the second time.
